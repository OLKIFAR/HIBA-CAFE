What really happened

In July, rent and milk went up, so Hiba cut her wage bill by €900 a month: two people on the morning shift instead of three.

With fewer hours, her two most experienced baristas, Ana and Tomas, left at the end of August. Hiba hired beginners: “No experience needed.”

Two beginners at the busiest time of the day made the line crawl. About 38 students a day walked out of the line, and many stopped coming in the morning at all. Mornings fell 71%. The rest of the day barely moved.

FastBean got the blame, but it only opens at 10:00. The bus stop moved, but just as many people still walk past. Corner Cup is still the cheapest café on the street, and the beans never changed.

The best fix: rebuild a full, trained morning team (fix 05). It costs about €1,200 a month. The empty mornings cost Hiba about €8,000 a month.

Why “Morning service got slow” scores 5, not 10: the slow line is real, but it's the result. The cause is why the line got slow.

        Say it · lead facilitator

        1. Today you're consultants. Corner Cup, a café next to the university, sells 40% less than last year. Find the real reason and the one fix the owner, Hiba, should pay for.
        2. Scan the code. Type your name and your team.
        3. First, 3 minutes alone: read the case and make a gut call. No talking.
        4. Then 10 minutes as a team: open up to 5 of the 12 exhibits and lock the reason. Lock early for +5 or +3. A 6th exhibit costs 2 points.
        5. Then 3 minutes to choose one fix. The exact right fix is worth 8.

        While they read

        • No talking for these 3 minutes.
        • Teams of 4 or 5. Online players can form their own team on a separate call.
        • Online host: keep the class call muted.

        While teams investigate

        • Say "go": everyone in a team presses Start at the same time.
        • Teams agree before opening an exhibit. Everyone opens the same one on their own phone.
        • At 4:00 left: "Lock now for +5." At 2:00 left: "Last call for +3."
        • Online team: one person shares their screen on their own call.

        While teams choose a fix

        • Each team's 3-minute fix timer starts the moment it locks its reason.
        • Eight fixes, each with a cost and how fast it works. One is exactly right (+8). Two are half fixes (+4).
        • When every team has locked its fix, go to the reveal.

        Reveal

        After the reveal, everyone saves their result card (PNG or PDF), then gives feedback.

        Scoreboard

        Ask each team for its score (out of 25) and type it in.

          Debrief · about 90 seconds per question

          1. What happened? Who blamed FastBean, the prices or the bus stop in their gut call? What changed your mind?
          2. How did your team work? How did you choose your 5 exhibits? Did anyone's idea get ignored?
          3. What does it tell us about teams at work? The loud rumours weren't the answer. The quiet numbers were.
          4. What will you do differently? Find where it breaks before asking why. Check a fix against the cause, its cost and its speed.

          Leadership angle: Hiba's own €900 saving cost her about €8,000 a month. Nobody suspected the owner.
